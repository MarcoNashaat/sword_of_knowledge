How to play
  • The world has 12 territories. Players place their castles in color order: red, then blue, then green.
  • Claim phase: all three players answer the same question at once. Each correct player claims a free territory next to land they already own. The picking order rotates every round. When fewer than 3 free territories remain, the question becomes numeric and only the closest guess picks.
  • Battle phase: every player gets 5 turns. On your turn, attack a territory next to yours that belongs to an opponent. Attacker and defender answer a question. If only one answers correctly, that player takes or keeps the territory. If both miss, nothing changes. If both are correct, a numeric question decides it and the closest guess wins.
  • Each castle has 3 towers. Every question the attacker wins knocks one down. If the defender wins a question, the attack stops and destroyed towers stay destroyed. A player with a damaged castle can attack as normal. Instead of attacking, they may spend a turn repairing: solo questions, one tower per correct answer, until they miss. Knocking down all three towers captures the castle and every territory its owner holds.
  • Scoring: a castle is worth 1,000 points and every other territory 300. Capturing a castle adds its 1,000 to your score. After the 5th turn of every player, the highest score wins.
